1. What cookies we use

Essential (always on)Keeps you logged in, remembers your shopping cart, and protects forms from cross-site attacks. The site can't function without these.
Analytics (optional)Helps us understand which pages are useful, only active if you accept it below.

2. Your choice

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or decline optional analytics cookies. Essential cookies can't be declined individually, since the site relies on them to work — but you can clear all cookies at any time through your browser settings, which will simply log you out and empty your cart.

3. Third parties

If enabled, our analytics provider (Google Analytics) may set its own cookies to measure site usage in aggregate. We don't use cookies for third-party advertising or retargeting.

4. Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you view, delete, and block cookies through their settings menu. Blocking essential cookies will prevent checkout and account login from working correctly.
