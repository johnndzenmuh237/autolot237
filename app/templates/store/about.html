Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy a car online without visiting the showroom first?

Yes. Every listing has full specs and real photos, and you can chat with our AI assistant or on WhatsApp with any questions. You can pay online and arrange delivery, or reserve a car online and pay when you pick it up in person.

Do you accept payment in installments or as a deposit?

Yes. At checkout you can pay the full amount, pay a partial deposit and settle the remaining balance later, or choose to pay on delivery. Your remaining balance is always visible in your order and account.

Is every car inspected before it's listed?

Yes, every vehicle is mechanically checked before it goes up for sale, and used cars carry a 30-day mechanical warranty from handover.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Mobile Money (MTN and Orange), bank transfer, and cash at the showroom.

Can I get a refund if I change my mind?

If you haven't paid yet, you can cancel your order at no cost. If you've paid a deposit or in full before handover, contact us and we'll handle it case by case — see our Refund Policy for full details.

Do you deliver cars outside Douala?

Yes — choose delivery at checkout and provide your address and city; our team will confirm delivery details and any distance-based arrangements with you directly.
