Last updated: {{ current_year }}. This policy explains what personal information {{ business_name }} collects, why, and how it's protected. It applies to this website and to information collected during in-person visits to our showroom.

1. What we collect

When you browse our site, we don't collect anything beyond standard technical logs. When you take an action — placing an order, submitting a test-drive request, chatting with us, or creating an account — we collect only what's needed to complete that action:

  • Full name, phone number, and (optionally) email address
  • Delivery address and city, if you choose delivery instead of showroom pickup
  • Order details: which vehicle, payment method chosen, and payment amounts recorded
  • Messages you send through our WhatsApp button or AI chat widget
  • An account password, only if you choose to create one

We do not collect government ID numbers, financial account numbers, or payment card details through this website — payments are arranged directly with our sales team via Mobile Money, bank transfer, or in person.

2. Why we collect it

We use your information only to: process and fulfill your order, contact you about your order or a test drive, respond to questions you send us, and maintain accurate sales records for our own business accounting. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Who can see it

Your order and contact details are visible to our own staff (sales team and management) for the purpose of fulfilling your order. We do not share your information with outside companies for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to keep you logged in and remember your cart. If analytics tracking is enabled, it only activates after you accept it in the cookie banner. See our Cookies Policy for details.

5. How long we keep it

Order and payment records are kept for our business accounting requirements. Account information is kept until you ask us to delete it.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to show you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by contacting us using the details below. If you have an account, you can also just sign in and review your order history directly.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to {{ business_email }} or via WhatsApp at {{ business_phone }}.
